About Us

This site is the second assignment for COMP 3512 (Web Development 2) at Mount Royal University, taken with professor Randy Connolly.

The site uses vanilla HTML, CSS, and Javascript. The site is hosted on a personal server, inside a docker-compose with a simple nginx reverse proxy. Domain routing is provided by a cloudflared tunnel linked to a personal domain.

This assignment was created by Konstantin Kirikeza.
